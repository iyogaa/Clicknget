In Commercial Insurance, businesses have to submit their Loss Runs. Loss Runs contain claims with descriptions of the incidents. 
These incident descriptions need to be categorized into accident categories. 
Following are the categories with their descriptions.

Categories | Description
Struck while Parked | Insured Vehicle (IV) was struck when it was parked
Insured hit in rear | Insured Vehicle (IV) was hit by the other vehicle (OV) or claimant vehicle (CV) in back or rear
Insured hit by other vehicle | Insured Vehicle (IV) was hit by the other vehicle (OV) or claimant vehicle (CV) other than in back or rear
Other vehicle lost control | Insured or Insured Vehicle (IV) event due to other vehicle losing control
Cut in and out sideswipe | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit the other vehicle (OV) or claimant vehicle (CV) at side while changing lanes
Intersection | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit the other vehicle (OV) or claimant vehicle (CV) while turning left, right, or making a U-turn
Insured backing | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit a car or some object while backing the car
Hit parked vehicle | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit a parked vehicle
External object | An external object damaged the Insured Vehicle (IV) or driver
Fell on/off vehicle | Some object fell on or off the vehicle and damaged the Insured Vehicle (IV) or driver or claimant vehicle (CV)
Head-on collision | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit the other vehicle (OV) or claimant vehicle (CV) head-on or in front
Hit animal | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit an animal
Hit claimant in rear | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit the other vehicle (OV) or claimant vehicle (CV) in back or rear
Hit pedestrian/cyclist/other | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit a pedestrian, cyclist, or someone else
Hit stationary object | Insured Vehicle (IV) hit a stationary object
Theft of vehicle | Insured Vehicle (IV) was stolen
Theft of parts | Insured Vehicle (IV) parts were stolen
Fire | Insured Vehicle (IV) was damaged by fire
Wind | Insured Vehicle (IV) was damaged by wind, water, thunderstorm, or cyclone
Insured lost control | Insured Vehicle (IV) lost control
Parking brake not set | Parking brake was not set by Insured Vehicle (IV)
Tire burst | Insured Vehicle (IV) tire burst
Tire came off | Insured Vehicle (IV) tire came off
Vehicle Jackknifing | Insured Vehicle (IV) caught in Jackknifing, IV trailer swings out or folds onto itself
Collision with Other Vehicle | Insured Vehicle (IV) collided or hit another vehicle (OV) or claimant vehicle (CV)
Multi Vehicle accident | Insured Vehicle (IV) was caught in a multi-vehicle accident
Other | Insured Vehicle (IV) was damaged by something else not mentioned above

Examples:
SNo | Description | Category

24. | IV was damaged by an unknown cause not listed above. | Other

Note: Above are just the examples, you may find more variations, Categorise accordingly.
